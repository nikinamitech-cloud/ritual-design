## Edits made
- `suit.jpg`: cropped to remove a brand label on the collar.
- `hearse.jpg`, `bus.jpg`: licence plates blurred.
- `mood-lilies.jpg`: cropped to 3:2.
- All files: re-encoded as JPEG (keeping the embedded colour profile) and resized. The 1600 px files are 350 KB or smaller; the `mood-*` files are 2000 px and 500 KB or smaller.
